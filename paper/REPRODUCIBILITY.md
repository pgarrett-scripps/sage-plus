## Search evidence

The working repository keeps the complete refresh under
`runs/paper-refresh-20260920/`. The `source/` directory began as an archive of
the beta 6 source tag. Its analysis and manuscript changes accompany the new
paper. The `evidence/` and `extension/` directories retain every original
comparative attempt. Any missing identification or entrapment cell receives at
most one separately retained retry in `retries/`. No failed timing or worker
scaling trial is replaced. The selected analysis directories link to the
recorded attempts without altering their files.

`analysis/data/execution.json` records all comparison attempts, outcomes, and
hashes of their result records. Failed attempts remain part of the completion
denominator. Resource summaries are conditional on successful completion.
Input and output verification is recorded in
`../benchmarks/scientific-results/20260920/evidence-verification.json`.

The shared spectra, reference databases, acquisition receipts, and construction
seeds remain under `/data/sage-plus-scientific/20260914`. Reuse of these inputs
preserves the study selection and pairing. It does not reuse older Sage Plus
search results. All beta.6 comparisons use the same beta.6 executable.
The records are local evidence, not a public deposition.

## Reproduce the analysis

The runners are `benchmarks/refresh_paper.py` and
`benchmarks/finish_paper_refresh.py` in the retained source directory. They
freeze commands, executable identities, configuration, input hashes, output
hashes, and process outcomes. They run searches sequentially. Use a new run
directory for a new executable or changed settings.

The reduction reads canonical results through the common scientific metric
functions. Report collectors derive identification disagreements, strict MS2
confirmation, LFQ ratios and coverage, PTM acceptance, and worker scaling.
Matched FDP analysis retains complete peptide q-value ties and audits every
selected threshold against the direct conservative paired estimator.

The mass-offset matrix and named-attachment fixtures use the same Sage Plus
binary. Their raw records are in `mass-offset/` and `named-modifications/`.
The synthesis-consistency diagnostic requires spectrum, peptide, and
localization q-values at most one percent. The file-to-library mapping remains
unaudited and these diagnostics do not establish empirical localization-error
calibration.

## Build the paper

Run these commands in the manuscript directory:

```bash
just assets
just fmt
just docx
just paper
just verify
just check-stats-deep
```

The build regenerates every figure, table, and prose statistic from retained
summaries. It does not launch searches. Figures use common colors and typography,
with 360 dpi PNG files and scalable SVG companions. The attachment schematic is
generated by `analysis/scripts/gen_modification_model_figure.py`.

For this desktop refresh, Word export uses the bundled Python runtime through
an isolated temporary environment. Both PDF and Word renderings are inspected
before delivery. Final manuscript rendering follows the measured searches. The workstation is
not isolated from ordinary background activity.
